Learn Amharic Fidel — ፊደል

Learn each Fidel letter, its reading sound, an example word, and Bible-related vocabulary.

How to study: Read each card from top to bottom. Tap 🔊 to hear the Fidel letter or word. Audio depends on whether your phone or browser has an Amharic voice installed. Some historical Fidel forms are rare in modern Amharic, so the lesson marks them clearly rather than using invented words.

To record, allow microphone access when your browser asks. Recordings are saved in this browser on this device. Use Download to save an audio file to your computer, then upload it to GitHub to share it.
